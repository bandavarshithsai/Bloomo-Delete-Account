Request account deletion

Instructions for Bloomo customers, shop owners and delivery partners.

How to request deletion

You can request deletion of your Bloomo account and associated personal data by contacting our privacy support email address:

bandavarshithsai@gmail.com

Email an account deletion request

In your request, include the phone number or email address registered to your Bloomo account and your account role (customer, shop owner or delivery partner). Do not include your password, OTP, payment PIN or other authentication secrets.

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What happens after you request deletion?

After verifying your request, Bloomo will review the account and associated information and process deletion in accordance with applicable legal requirements and operational needs. We will contact you if we need additional information or if some records must be retained.

Processing time: Bloomo's deletion processing timeframe has not yet been confirmed. The publisher must set and follow a specific timeframe before relying on this page for app-store submission.

What information may be deleted?

Subject to verification and applicable retention requirements, deletion may cover your account profile and personal information associated with your account, such as contact details, saved addresses and other account-linked data.

Information that may be retained

Some records may need to be retained for legal compliance, accounting, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution or completion of outstanding orders. Examples may include relevant transaction/order records and records needed to document the deletion request. Retained information will be limited to what is necessary and handled according to applicable requirements.

Need help?

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact bandavarshithsai@gmail.com.

Important for the Bloomo publisher: This page currently provides an email-based request route. It does not itself delete accounts or data. Before submitting to Google Play, implement and test an operational deletion process that deletes or de-identifies applicable Firebase Authentication accounts and associated Firestore/Storage data, handles outstanding orders and legally required retention, and confirms completion to the requester. Also add an account-deletion option inside the app if users can create accounts.
